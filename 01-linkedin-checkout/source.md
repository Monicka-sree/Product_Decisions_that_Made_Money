# Sources — LinkedIn Checkout

## Primary Source

Jon Jordan — LinkedIn Checkout Revenue Growth Case Study

https://www.jonjordanixd.com/checkout-page-revenue-growth

## Key Information Taken From the Source

- The initiative involved 30 checkout experiments.
- Experiments were prioritized based on potential impact and effort.
- The case study reports an estimated $48M revenue uplift after four months from completed experiments.

## Important Evidence Notes

The publicly available case study does not specify:

- Exact number of users involved
- Exact traffic split for each experiment
- Whether all 30 experiments ran simultaneously
- Exact methodology used to calculate the $48M estimate

Therefore, these details should not be presented as confirmed facts.

## How I Use This Source

The LinkedIn post describes the $48M figure as **estimated revenue uplift** and avoids making claims about experimental sample size or simultaneous testing that are not publicly documented.
