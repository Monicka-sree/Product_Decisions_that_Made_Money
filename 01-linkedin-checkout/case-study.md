# Product Decisions That Made Money — #01

## LinkedIn Checkout

### Business Problem

LinkedIn wanted to improve revenue from its checkout experience.

### Product Approach

Instead of relying on one large checkout redesign, the team worked on 30 experiments and prioritized opportunities based on potential impact and effort.

### Experiment

The experiments were tested with users in the checkout experience to understand which changes created an impact.

The publicly available case study does not specify the exact number of users involved, whether all 30 experiments ran simultaneously, or the exact traffic split for each experiment.

### Result

**$48M estimated revenue uplift**

The case study reports that the completed experiments were estimated to have generated approximately $48M in revenue uplift after four months.

### PM Takeaway

You don't always need one massive product change.

A series of well-prioritized experiments can uncover meaningful business impact.
