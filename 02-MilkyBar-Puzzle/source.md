## Impact & Evidence Note

This case study distinguishes between **documented evidence** and **interpretation**.

### Documented

The official Nestlé and VML sources support the role of imagination, curiosity, learning, play and interactive moulds in the Milkybar strategy. VML also explicitly connects the challenge with brand relevance and growth. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

### Not publicly established

A specific revenue figure directly caused by the interactive puzzle/mould experience was not identified in the sources used for this case study.

Therefore, no specific revenue uplift is attributed to the decision.

**Principle:**

> If the number isn't supported by evidence, don't put a number on it.
