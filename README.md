# Product Decisions That Made Money 

A personal learning series where I study interesting product decisions and try to understand the **business thinking behind them**.

This repository contains the research behind my LinkedIn series:

**"Product Decisions That Made Money"**

The goal is not just to find interesting products.

I want to understand:

- What problem was the company trying to solve?
- What product decision did they make?
- What was the business impact?
- How was the decision tested or measured?
- What numbers are actually publicly documented?
- What can I learn from it as someone learning Product Management?

---

## 🔍 What Each Case Study Covers

Each case study follows a simple structure:

### 1. Business Problem
What user or business problem was the product team trying to solve?

### 2. Product Decision
What did the team change, build, test, or prioritize?

### 3. Experiment / Approach
How did they test or implement the idea?

### 4. Business Impact
What happened to metrics such as:

- Revenue
- Conversion
- Retention
- Engagement
- Cost
- ROI

### 5. Evidence & Limitations
What can actually be verified from publicly available sources?

I will clearly distinguish between:
- Documented facts
- Reported results
- Estimates
- My own interpretation

### 6. PM Takeaway
What product-management lesson did I take away from the case?
