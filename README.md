# Product Decisions That Made Money 💰

### By Monicka Sree

> I'm an aspiring Product Manager, and I'm learning to look at products beyond their features.

This repository is my collection of **real product case studies** that I find interesting while learning Product Management.

I’m particularly interested in one question:

**How do product decisions translate into business impact?**

So instead of only studying what a product does, I’m exploring the decisions behind it — the problem, the experiment, the metric, and the money.

---

## What is this series?

**Product Decisions That Made Money** is my learning series where I pick an interesting product decision and dig into:

**Problem → Decision → Experiment → Result → PM takeaway**

The focus is on cases where companies have publicly shared measurable outcomes such as:

- Revenue
- Conversion
- Retention
- ROI
- Cost reduction
- Engagement

Whenever possible, I want to understand the **actual numbers behind the decision**, rather than simply saying that a feature was "successful."

## How I treat the numbers

I don't want this repository to become a collection of impressive-sounding statistics.

So I try to separate:

**What is documented**  
from  
**what is reported**  
from  
**what I am interpreting.**

If a number is an estimate, I will call it an **estimate**.

If important details aren't publicly available, I'll mention that too.

---

## Why am I doing this?

I'm trying to build the habit of thinking like a PM:

**Who is the user?**  
**What problem are we solving?**  
**Why this solution?**  
**How would we test it?**  
**What metric should move?**  
**And ultimately — does it create business value?**

This series is my way of practising that thinking using products that already exist.
